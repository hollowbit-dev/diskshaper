Privacy

Updated 11 November 2026

Diskshaper scans files on your computer and never sends anything anywhere. The app makes no network requests. This website has no analytics and no cookies.

What the app reads

File names, sizes and dates in your user folders, app caches and game libraries, and the game lists Steam, Epic, Heroic and CrossOver keep. To find duplicates it reads files to compare their contents. All of this stays in memory on your computer and is gone when you close the app.

What the app changes

Nothing, until you press the clean button. Then it moves the items you ticked to the Trash or Recycle Bin using your system’s own move-to-Trash function.

Network

The app makes no network requests. There are no updates checks, analytics or crash reports. Buttons like “Uninstall in Steam” open your launcher on your computer.

This website

Hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs. We add no analytics, cookies or trackers. The duplicate photo finder runs in your browser and never uploads your photos.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
