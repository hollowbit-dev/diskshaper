How to clear the DaVinci Resolve cache

DaVinci Resolve stores its render cache, optimized media and proxies in a CacheClip folder inside the working folder set in its preferences. These are all made from your original footage and can be made again. In Resolve, use Playback, Delete Render Cache, All. To catch the rest, and caches from old projects, scan with Diskshaper.

Updated 11 November 2026 · Hollowbit Studio

Download for Mac Download for Windows

Step by step

  1. Quit DaVinci ResolveFiles in use cannot be moved.
  2. Download Diskshaper and press ScanIt finds CacheClip and ProxyMedia folders in your Movies or Videos folder and next to your projects.
  3. Open Video editingResolve’s caches are listed under Video editing caches and Render previews in project folders.
  4. Move them to the Trash or Recycle BinResolve rebuilds what it needs when you play the timeline.

Where Resolve keeps its cache

The location is set per project under Project Settings, Master Settings, Working Folders, and defaults to the first drive in Preferences, System, Media Storage. Inside it, Resolve makes a CacheClip folder.

The manual way

Open the project and choose Playback, Delete Render Cache, then All or Unused. Do this for each project. Finished projects you never open again keep their cache until you delete the folder yourself.

Render cache vs optimized media vs proxies

Render cache holds pre-rendered effects and grades. Optimized media and proxies are lighter copies of your footage for smooth playback. All three are made from your originals, so they are safe to remove for finished projects.

Questions

Is deleting the CacheClip folder safe?

Yes, for any project. Resolve treats it as disposable and recreates it.

Will it delete my footage?

No. Your original media is not in the cache folder. Diskshaper never marks original footage as Safe.

How much space can it free?

On an editing machine, often 20 to 100 GB.
