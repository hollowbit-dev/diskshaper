**Space map, more game launchers, and a running total of what you've cleared.**

- **Space map.** See what's eating your storage at a glance: every folder as a block sized by how much space it takes. Click a folder to zoom in, use the breadcrumbs to go back up, right-click anything to show it in Finder or File Explorer. Map your own files (about half a minute) or the whole drive. The map only reads: cleaning still goes through Scan, which knows what is safe to remove.
- **More game launchers.** Caches and logs from Battle.net, Riot Client, the EA app, Ubisoft Connect, GOG Galaxy and the Rockstar Launcher, next to Steam and Epic.
- **Graphics driver installers (Windows).** NVIDIA and AMD leave a full copy of every driver installer behind, often several GB. Diskshaper finds them.
- **Crash reports.** Crash dumps and error reports from apps and games, including Unreal Engine games.
- **Browser and chat caches (Windows).** Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera GX, Discord, Spotify, Slack, Teams and VS Code.
- **Cleared so far.** The start screen shows how much Diskshaper has cleared for you in total (kept only on your computer), with a picture you can share.

**Downloads:** Windows (`Setup-…-win-x64.exe`), Mac with Apple Silicon (`-mac-arm64.dmg`) or Intel (`-mac-x64.dmg`).
Not yet signed with paid certificates: on Windows click **More info › Run anyway**; on a Mac right-click Diskshaper in Applications and choose **Open** the first time.
