First release of Diskshaper, a free storage cleaner for Mac and Windows.

- Scans your drive in a few seconds and sorts what it finds into Everyone, Gaming and Video editing.
- Gaming: shader caches, Steam and Epic leftovers, unfinished downloads, launcher caches, games you have not played in 3 months (Steam, Epic, Heroic), CrossOver bottles.
- Video editing: Premiere, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut and CapCut caches, render previews next to projects, old auto-saves, duplicate footage, old exports.
- Everyone: app caches, developer tool downloads, old installers and zips, duplicate photos, huge forgotten files, old screen recordings.
- Plain Safe and Check first labels. Only Safe items are ticked for you.
- Everything moves to the Trash or Recycle Bin. Nothing is deleted outright.

**Downloads:** Mac Apple Silicon (`-mac-arm64.dmg`), Mac Intel (`-mac-x64.dmg`), Windows (`Setup-...-win-x64.exe`).
The app is not signed with a paid certificate: on Mac, right-click it and choose Open the first time; on Windows, choose More info, Run anyway.
